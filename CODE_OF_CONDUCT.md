# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Kerzon Skills is committed to a welcoming and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus critique on ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep discussions on-topic and useful to contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to: `aibarrakerzon@gmail.com`

All reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.
